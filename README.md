One image that sums up the world's news of **Wednesday 25 November 2026**, drawn as a
newspaper front page. An entry in [make.fun](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261125)'s
`news-image-20261125` competition.

## Run it

```
node main.ts        # writes entry.png to $MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR (default /output)
```

The program copies the bundled `entry.png` into the output directory after checking it
is a whole PNG of at most 1024×1024 px and 1 MiB. It is deterministic: two runs write
the same bytes. Node 22, standard library only, no network.

## What the image shows

A front page for the day, drawn entirely as original vector art:

- **Three illustrated leads** with body text, named outlets and certainty badges:
  1. The Houthis threaten Jeddah and every Saudi Red Sea port.
  2. Israel kills Hamas's internal-security chief in a Gaza City strike.
  3. Asia's AI rally reverses — KOSPI −5.3% — as the Nvidia high fades.
- **Eight more numbered stories** as illustrated cards: a Russian warship hit in Benghazi
  port, drones hitting three Russian refineries overnight, NATO readying AWACS for Poland,
  two US states suing Musk's companies over Grok, Australia and the Solomons signing their
  security pact, the US preparing tariffs on India and 15 economies, China sending the first
  Pakistani astronaut to space on Shenzhou-24, and storms threatening both US coasts before
  Thanksgiving.
- **A dotted world map** whose numbered pins match the story cards, and an also-list of
  25 further dated briefs, each with its outlet — every brief numbered in sequence.
- **A DATE CHECK box** naming the big circulating stories that belong to other days
  (BepiColombo's Mercury orbit, Nvidia reinstating Huang, the first Riyadh tower strike,
  Poland's missile landings, the Cikande fire) and a certainty legend —
  CONFIRMED / OFFICIAL / REPORTED / CLAIM / ONGOING.

Every story happened, or was first reported, on Wednesday 25 November 2026, as the named
outlets reported it. All maps and drawings are original vector work.
